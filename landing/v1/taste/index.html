Questions

Is Sara an AI?

No. Sara is a numbered menu that answers the same way every time. You stay in control of every invoice and link.

Does Sara message my customers on WhatsApp?

No. Sara only replies to you. Customers receive confirmation, reschedule and cancellation emails, plus a reminder 24 hours before.

How do I get paid?

Through Paystack split payments into your business's own subaccount. Mono links your bank account for settlement.

Will it clash with my Google Calendar?

No. Sync is two-way, so busy time on your calendar is never offered to customers.

Can I run it from Instagram?

Yes. The same menu works in your Instagram chat.
