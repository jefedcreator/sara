Questions owners ask first

Is Sara an AI chatbot?

No. Sara is a guided menu. You reply with a number and Sara answers the same way every time. Send "menu" or "0" to go back.

Will Sara message my customers on WhatsApp?

Never. Sara only replies to you. Customers get emails: confirmation, reschedule, cancellation, and a reminder 24 hours before their appointment.

Where does the money go?

Paystack split payments settle straight into your business's own subaccount. Mono links your bank account for settlement.

I already use Google Calendar.

Good. Sync runs both ways: confirmed bookings are added, cancellations removed and reschedules moved, and your busy time is never offered to customers.

Can I use Instagram instead of WhatsApp?

Yes. The same numbered menu works in your Instagram chat.
