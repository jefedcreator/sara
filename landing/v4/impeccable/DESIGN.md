## Type

- **Figtree** 400–700: UI and body.

## Shape
14px buttons (not pills), 20–22px cards, 12px chips.

## Signature
The sticky clock column (07:12 → 20:30) with each hour proving one Sara reply. Hero card settles, "5" arrives, then the reply, then a slow 9s drift.

## Hero slot
`.scene-photo` full-bleed, 16:7 desktop, 4:5 phone crop; the white chat card floats centred on it.
