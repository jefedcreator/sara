Questions

Is Sara an AI?

No. Sara is a guided menu with fixed replies. Reply with a number and you get the same kind of answer every time.

Will Sara message my customers?

Only by email: confirmations, reschedules, cancellations and a reminder 24 hours before. Sara never messages customers on WhatsApp.

Can I use Instagram?

Yes. The same menu works in your Instagram chat.

Where does the money go?

Through Paystack split payments into your business's own subaccount, with your bank linked by Mono.
