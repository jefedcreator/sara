# Sara v3 · Impeccable · Warm Green Nature-Finance

Documented from the built page.

## World
Calm money. Unhurried, warm, the day already handled; proof arrives as small kind messages.

## Type
- **Switzer** 400–700 for headlines and UI (bold sans, tracking −0.035 to −0.04em).
- **Sentient Italic** 400/500 for exactly one emphasis word per headline.

## Shape
28px cards, pill controls, arch-topped hero photo (260px top radii), one oversized soft corner per panel (invoice 180px, closing card 220px).

## Ornament
Hand-authored four-point sparkle (`#spark` symbol) as brand mark, list marker and band decoration. Soft blob shapes at two hero corners.

## Motion
Hero copy rises, photo settles, payment toast lands last (0.9s). Blocks rise once on scroll. Reduced motion respected.

## Hero slot
`.photo` 4:5 portrait, arch top; `.toast` overlaps its left edge.
