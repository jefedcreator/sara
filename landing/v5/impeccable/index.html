FAQ

Is Sara an AI chatbot?

No. It is a guided menu. The same number always does the same thing. Send "menu" or "0" to go back.

Do I need a laptop?

No. Setup and daily use happen in the chat. You sign in once with Google, Facebook or Instagram through a one-time link.

Will my customers get WhatsApp messages?

No. Sara only replies to you. Customers get emails.
