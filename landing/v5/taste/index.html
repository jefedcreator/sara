How it works

Four steps. After that, the business runs from a numbered menu.

  1. 01

    Message Sara on WhatsApp and connect your business with a one-time link.

  2. 02

    Add your services, prices and working hours.

  3. 03

    Reply "3" to get a booking link and drop it in your bio or a chat.

  4. 04

    The customer picks a slot and pays by Paystack; the receipt and your notification arrive on their own.

Yes. The same menu works in your Instagram chat.

No. Sara only replies to you. Customers get emails.
