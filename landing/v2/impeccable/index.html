FAQ

Is Sara AI?

No. It is a numbered menu with fixed replies. Same input, same output, every time.

Does Sara work in Instagram DMs?

Yes. The same menu and the same numbers run in your Instagram chat.

Will my customers get WhatsApp messages from Sara?

No. Sara only replies to you. Customers receive confirmation, reschedule and cancellation emails, and a reminder 24 hours before.

Where is money held?

Nowhere in between. Paystack split payments settle into your business's own subaccount; Mono links your bank for settlement.

What syncs with Google Calendar?

Confirmed bookings are added, cancellations removed and reschedules moved. Your busy time is subtracted from what customers can book.
